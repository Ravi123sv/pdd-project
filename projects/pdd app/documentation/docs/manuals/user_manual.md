# NeuroSignal AI — Clinical User Manual

## 🏥 1. System Overview
NeuroSignal AI is your primary workstation for high-fidelity biometric acquisition. It is designed to simplify the monitoring of ECG (Heart) and EEG (Brain) signals through automated AI assistance.

## 🔑 2. Getting Started
### 2.1 Authentication
- **Institutional Access**: Enter the **Clinical Access Key** provided by your administrator. This key is required to unlock the workstation and your local encrypted vault.
- **Individual Access**: Sign in using your medical email and password. You may also use the "Email Link" for passwordless entry via your institutional Gmail.

## 💓 3. Live Monitoring
### 3.1 Initializing Acquisition
1. Navigate to the **Dashboard** and click **INITIALIZE ACQUISITION**.
2. Enter the Patient's **MRN** and name.
3. Select the test type (ECG or EEG).
4. Click **LINK HARDWARE** to connect your sensors via Bluetooth or USB.

### 3.2 Signal Interpretation
- **Neural Interpretation**: The AI consultant in the bottom console provides real-time observations (e.g., "Normal Sinus Rhythm").
- **Spectral Analytics**: Toggle the chart icon in the monitor to see the frequency breakdown of EEG waves.
- **Manual Flagging**: Use the **FLAG** button to mark any clinical events (e.g., patient movement) for the final report.

## 📂 4. Data Export
- Navigate to the **Export Vault** to generate professional PDF reports.
- Use **EMR Sync** to automatically transmit findings to the hospital's central records system.

## 🚨 5. Emergency Protocol
- If a patient enters a critical state, click the **RED ALERT** button on the Dashboard. This broadcasts a high-priority notification to the entire clinical team across the hospital network.
